How to read a diagram

The vertical lines are strings. The left line is the low E string, and the right line is the high E string. Horizontal lines are frets.

Make chords ring clearly

  • Press close behind the fret.
  • Curve your fingers.
  • Keep your thumb relaxed.

Practice changes

Choose two chords. Switch every four beats at 60 BPM. Add speed only when the change is smooth and every note rings.
